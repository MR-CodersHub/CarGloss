Methodical Precision

Our 18-Step Process

Every vehicle undergoes our certified procedural sequence — zero shortcuts, complete thoroughness.

Phase 1: Exterior Decontamination (Steps 1–9)

1
Pre-inspection & paint depth audit: Measure clearcoat thickness and log existing micro-scratches.
2
Wheel wells & brake dust soak: Acid-free iron dissolver softens heavy rotor dust.
3
High-density snow foam cannon bath: 5-minute dwell encapsulates loose grit.
4
Touchless pure water pressure rinse: Washes away lifted dirt particles safely.
5
Two-bucket dual grit-guard contact wash: Ultra-soft chenille mitts glide effortlessly.
6
Iron & fallout chemical bleed: Neutralizes industrial fallout and rail dust embedded in panels.
7
Synthetic clay bar treatment: Removes residual tar, sap, and overspray for a glass finish.
8
Forced warm filtered air dry: Evicts trapped water from mirrors, emblems, and window seals.
9
Tire satin silicone glaze: Nourishes rubber sidewalls with long-lasting UV repeller.

Phase 2: Interior Sanitization (Steps 10–18)

10
High-lift HEPA vacuum extraction: Deep carpet fibers, crevices, under-seat tracks, and trunk.
11
Dry-steam console sanitization: Hospital-grade 280°F dry steam lifts drink spills and bacteria.
12
Air vent agitation & blow-out: Soft boar-hair brush purges hidden dust from louvers.
13
Leather deep cleansing & conditioning: Restores natural suppleness and factory matte sheen.
14
Door jambs, hinges & fuel door wipe: Degreases door shuts and factory rubber gaskets.
15
Tint-safe interior optical glass polish: Leaves windshield and mirrors with zero haze.
16
Pedals, floor anchors & seatbelt wipe: Eliminates grease from high-touch driver controls.
17
Cabin botanical air refresh mist: Hypoallergenic, non-toxic signature scent neutralizer.
18
Certified 30-point walkthrough inspection: Complete digital verification before customer handover.
