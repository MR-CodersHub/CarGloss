Seasonal Field Guides

Year-Round Automotive Protection

Environmental contaminants change each season. Here is what our master detailers recommend for each quarter.

Spring Strategy

Pollen & Sap Decontamination

Pollen grains contain acidic enzymes that etch into warm clearcoats. Weekly two-bucket rinses and synthetic clay bar treatments prevent yellow staining.

Summer Strategy

UV Shielding & Bug Splatter

Bake-in temperatures dry dead insects onto front bumpers, degrading paint. Apply ceramic quartz spray sealant and conditioned leather UV block.

Autumn Strategy

Rain Repellency & Trim Care

Prepare wiper channels, cowl drains, and windshield glass with hydrophobic fluoropolymer coatings to ensure crystal clear night rain visibility.

Winter Strategy

Road Salt & Brine Neutralization

Calcium chloride accelerates chassis corrosion. Underbody wash blasts and polymer sealants create a sacrificial barrier against road salt damage.

Master Pro Tips

Common Myths & Pro Rules

Never Wash in Direct Sunlight

Water drops act like magnifying lenses, heating mineral deposits that chemically bake into your clearcoat. Always wash in shade or under canopy.

The Two-Bucket Grit Guard Rule

One bucket holds clean suds, the second holds rinse water with a grit separator. This traps abrasive grit at the bottom, eliminating 95% of swirl marks.

Top-to-Bottom Flow Sequence

The dirtiest parts of any car are the rocker panels and wheels. Clean upper glass and hoods first so grit doesn't migrate upwards onto delicate paint.
